# Decisions

> This is the part of your submission we read most carefully. Prose is fine—bullet
> points are fine too. Length isn't a virtue—roughly a page is plenty.

## What I built

A couple of sentences on the shape of what you built and why you structured it that way.

## Assumptions I made

Anything the spec didn't tell you that you had to decide for yourself. For each one:
what you assumed, and what you'd have asked if you could.

## Problems I found in the spec

Where the spec is unclear, incomplete, or argues with itself. For each one:

- what the problem is
- how you resolved it, and why
- what you'd change about the spec itself

## What I deliberately did not build

Things you decided were out of scope, and the reasoning. This is a real signal for us—knowing
what to leave out is most of the job.

## What I'd do with three more hours

Where you'd go next, in priority order.

## How I used AI, if I did

Briefly—what you delegated, what you kept, and anything it got wrong that you caught.
